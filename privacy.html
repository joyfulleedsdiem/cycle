Privacy & Disclaimer

Last updated: 25 September 2026

What this app is

This is a personal calendar that estimates hormonal cycle phases from dates you log yourself, so you can plan around them. It is a personal project, not a medical device or clinical tool.

Not medical advice

Phase and date estimates are calculated from simple averages of the dates you enter. They are not a diagnosis, not a guarantee of accuracy, and not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Do not use this app for contraception, fertility treatment decisions, or any medical purpose. If you have concerns about your cycle or health, speak with a doctor.

What data is collected, and where it lives

This app collects only what you type into it: period dates, cycle-length settings, plans/notes you add, and your colour preference. That data is written to your browser's local storage on your own device.

  • There is no account, login, or sign-up.
  • There is no server. Your entries are never transmitted anywhere — they never leave your device.
  • There is no analytics, advertising SDK, or third-party tracking of any kind.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, because no one but you ever has it.

Your controls

In Settings, you can export a full backup of your data as a file you keep yourself, import a backup, or permanently clear all data from this device. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data also deletes everything.

If you use this on more than one device

Local storage is per-device and per-browser. Data entered on your phone will not automatically appear on your laptop, and vice versa, unless you use Export/Import to move a backup file yourself.

Changes to this approach

If a future version of this app adds an account, cloud sync, or any feature that sends data off your device, that change will be described here before it's turned on, along with what data would be involved and why.
