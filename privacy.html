Privacy & Disclaimer

Last updated: 4 October 2026

What this app is

This is a personal calendar that estimates hormonal cycle phases from dates you log yourself, so you can plan around them. It is a personal project, not a medical device or clinical tool.

Not medical advice

Phase and date estimates are calculated from simple averages of the dates you enter. They are not a diagnosis, not a guarantee of accuracy, and not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Do not use this app for contraception, fertility treatment decisions, or any medical purpose. If you have concerns about your cycle or health, speak with a doctor.

What data is collected, and where it lives

This app collects what you type into it: your name, period dates, cycle-length settings, plans/notes you add, and your colour preference. Creating an account requires an email and password (or signing in with Google, which shares only your email with us, nothing else from your Google account).

Your data is stored in a private database record tied to your account, hosted by Firebase (a Google Cloud service). It is protected by access rules that only allow your own logged-in account to read or write it — not other users, and not us browsing the database by hand.

  • There is no analytics, advertising SDK, or third-party tracking of any kind.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party for marketing or any other purpose.
  • Your password is never stored by us in readable form — authentication is handled entirely by Firebase's own secure login system.
  • A cached copy of your data also sits in this browser's local storage, purely so the app loads instantly; the account record in the cloud is the authoritative copy.

Your controls

In Settings, you can export a full backup of your data as a file you keep yourself, import a backup, log out of your account on this device, or permanently delete your account's data and log out. Deleting your data removes your cloud record; it does not remove the underlying login (email/Google) itself, which stays registered unless you ask for it to be removed separately.

Using this on more than one device

Because your data lives on your account rather than only on one device, logging in with the same email/password (or Google account) on another phone or browser brings your data with you.

Changes to this approach

If a future version of this app changes how or where data is stored, that change will be described here before it takes effect, along with what data would be involved and why.
